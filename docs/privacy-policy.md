# CanaMastery Privacy Policy — Draft

**Status:** Draft for legal/product review before store publication.

CanaMastery is designed as an offline-first app and is intentionally built to avoid a user account, analytics, advertising identifiers, or a proprietary backend.

## Information stored on the device

The app can store locally:

- selected language;
- selected province or territory;
- study progress and completion status;
- review / spaced-repetition state;
- exam attempts and results;
- app preferences such as theme, text size, and reminders;
- local premium-entitlement cache.

These values are used to operate the app and are not intentionally uploaded to a CanaMastery server in this version.

## Purchases

Premium is sold through the platform store. Payment processing is handled by Google Play or Apple App Store according to the platform's own terms and privacy practices. CanaMastery does not receive or store a user's full payment-card details.

## Notifications

Study reminders, when enabled, are scheduled locally on the device. CanaMastery does not use remote push notifications in this version.

## Analytics and advertising

CanaMastery does not intentionally collect analytics events and does not display advertising in this version.

## Data deletion

Because user progress is stored locally, deleting the app and its local data removes the locally stored study state. Store purchase history remains subject to the platform's account and purchase systems.

## Children's privacy

CanaMastery is intended for general audiences and is an educational study application. The app does not intentionally request or collect personal profile information.

## Government affiliation

CanaMastery is not an official Government of Canada or IRCC application and does not represent that it can predict or reproduce the live exam.
