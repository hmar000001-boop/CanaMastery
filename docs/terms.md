# CanaMastery Terms of Use — Draft

**Status:** Draft for legal/product review before store publication.

CanaMastery is an educational preparation tool. It is not an official Government of Canada or IRCC product, and it does not guarantee a passing result on the citizenship test.

## Content

Study summaries and practice questions are educational materials written for CanaMastery. Official-source citations identify the Government of Canada material used as the factual reference. CanaMastery does not claim ownership of third-party government material merely by citing it.

Users must not attempt to extract, redistribute, resell, or republish the app's proprietary question database, summaries, software, or other protected assets except where permitted by applicable law.

## Premium entitlement

The premium entitlement is a one-time digital purchase configured through the relevant app store. The store's purchase, refund, cancellation, tax, and account rules apply in addition to these terms.

## No guarantee

CanaMastery content can become outdated when the official study guide changes. The product is designed to follow the currently published guide edition used by the content team, but users should always follow current instructions from IRCC for their own application and test.

## Limitation

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, CanaMastery is provided for educational use without a guarantee of availability, completeness, or suitability for a particular outcome.
