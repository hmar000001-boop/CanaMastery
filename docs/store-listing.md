## Full description

Prepare for the Canadian citizenship test with CanaMastery, an offline-first study app built for focused learning and realistic practice.

### Learn step by step

Review the major citizenship topics through short, easy-to-read lessons instead of reading a long reference guide from beginning to end.

### Practice with original questions

Train with practice questions linked to official Government of Canada source references. Explanations are written for learning and citations help you understand where the underlying fact comes from.

### Personalize your regional content

Choose your province or territory during onboarding. CanaMastery automatically shows regional questions for that jurisdiction so you can focus on the information relevant to where you live.

### Practice the exam format

Use a realistic mock-exam experience based on the published Canadian citizenship test format:

- 20 questions
- 45 minutes
- 15 correct answers required to pass
- Multiple-choice and true/false questions

### Offline by design

No account. No advertising. No analytics. Your study progress and preferences stay on your device in this version.

### Free to start

Start with a free selection of chapters and questions. Unlock the full learning and practice experience with a one-time premium purchase.

CanaMastery is an independent educational app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Canada or IRCC.
